Block 5 · Audit · the number you would have to sign

A threshold fitted on a hundred verdicts inherits their noise

The policy

Auto-accept a verdict when its score ≥ t; otherwise it goes to a human. Coverage is the share you accept, escape the share that is wrong and accepted anyway.

NB5 takes the smallest t holding error under 10 % on calibration.

Why that is not a guarantee

On cal the target is met by construction — that is where the threshold was fitted.

On test it can be exceeded: tuned on roughly a hundred verdicts, it carries their sampling noise with it.

Certify “at most 5 % of verdicts are wrong and unreviewed”. Is a threshold tuned on a hundred verdicts evidence for that?

NB5 section 6. A 5 % target left almost no coverage with this judge, hence 10 %.
